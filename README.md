## What she does

- **Lives on your desktop.** A tiny fox with an ema clock plaque who walks, naps, eats and reacts while you work.
- **Guards your focus.** Lock-in sessions, a focus timer and gentle nudges when distractions creep in.
- **Plans your study.** A study planner, subject tracking, a notebook per subject, practice books and a grade tracker, built around the IB Diploma.
- **Looks after the rest of your day.** Daily page, exercise tab, breathing exercises, a zen garden that grows with your focus, and screen time.
- **Talks with you.** Chat, offline voice chat and an anime fox voice.
- **Fast and easy to find your way.** Press Ctrl+K to jump to any page, setting or action, arrange the sidebar how you like, and pick an accent colour.
- **Free and offline where possible.** No account, no subscription.

## Install

1. Download **Zenko.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/AnimatedAFK/Zenko/releases/latest).
2. Run it. Zenko installs herself and keeps herself updated from the releases here.
