# Zenko changes

## v2026.11.25-zenko23

Zenko 23 lets your ema reminders plan their own study time, turns the Town square into a festival street, and links study sessions with Zenko Browser's focus mode. Your saved data and settings carry over.

- **Emas that plan themselves.** Hang a homework-like ema ("finish bio IA draft by friday", "revise chem organic", "maths past paper 1 on saturday") and Zenko finds study time for it in your free half hours (the Upcoming clock) before it's due, around your exam plan. She tells you when she planned it, and the sessions show on your timetable and in the Upcoming plan as "Ema: ...".
  - She guesses how long a task takes (an IA or essay 2 h, a past paper 1.5 h, homework 45 min, revision 1 h, reading 30 min) or uses what you write ("revise bio for 2 hours"). Say "bio IA takes 3 hours" to change it.
  - An ema with no date gets planned within three days. An ema at a set time ("study french at 5pm", "call mum at 6pm") keeps that time free instead.
  - When you tick off the last session she asks if she can take the ema down. Daily emas (like "drink water every day") are never planned.
  - Say "plan my emas" to see it all, or "don't plan call mum". Settings > Emas that plan themselves turns it off.
- **The Town square is a festival street now.** A little country town on festival night above the chat: machiya houses with lit lattices, strings of chochin lanterns that flicker, yatai stalls (takoyaki, goldfish scooping, candy apples, shaved ice, masks...), a yagura with a taiko drum, a crowd walking by in yukata, and fireworks. The sky follows the time of day.
  - **Everyone in the chat walks in the crowd** with their name above them, and new messages pop up as speech bubbles over their heads. You're there too, as a fox in a yukata. Right-click the street to pick your yukata colour or wear a fox mask; others see your look.
  - **Click the stalls:** feed Zenko takoyaki, scoop a goldfish (+1 koi bait), draw an omikuji fortune once a day, try the shooting gallery, buy a charm.
  - **Real festival dates.** The street dresses up for New Year, Setsubun, Valentine's Day, Hina-matsuri, Holi, hanami, Children's Day, Tanabata, summer matsuri, Obon (bon-odori dancers), Tsukimi, Halloween (pumpkins, witches, little ghosts, bats), Diwali (diyas, rangoli), Christmas (snow, a big tree, Santa hats) and New Year's Eve, with the seasons in between (autumn leaves right now). The banner says which festival is next. Right-click > Preview a festival to see any of them.
- **Study sessions turn on Zenko Browser's focus mode.** Start a focus session or lock-in and the browser's focus mode switches on at once; it switches off when the session ends. Distracting sites rest behind a shoji screen that slides shut, with your session and minutes left on a hanging ema.
- New chat commands: "plan my emas", "X takes 2 hours", "don't plan X", "festival".

## v2026.11.25-zenko22

Zenko 22 adds koi fishing, a much longer journey, lots more badges, the Town square and a real link with Zenko Browser. Your saved data and settings carry over.

- **Koi fishing in the Zen garden.** Cast a line in the pond (the 釣り · Koi fishing card, or say "go fishing"). When something bites, answer a question to reel it in: one of your due flashcards, or a question from a bank of about 110 (your six IB subjects, general knowledge and Japan). Harder questions and deeper water bring rarer fish.
  - 32 kinds of fish, from medaka and goldfish to ogon, kujaku, tanchō and the legendary Ryū and Kitsunebi. Each fish you catch has its own pattern and size. They're drawn as proper pixel art with scales, fins and sheen.
  - Some only bite at night, in rain or snow, in a season, or under a full moon.
  - A koi book with silhouettes of what you haven't caught yet, and up to 6 favourites swimming in your pond.
  - 3 free casts a day; focus time earns bait (one per 10 minutes) and 25-minute lock-ins earn golden bait. Study hours unlock better rods (Lacquer, Silver, Dragon) and lanterns that help at night.
- **The journey goes on after Japan.** When Zenko has visited all 18 Japanese places she carries on: Kyoto through the four seasons, Korea, China, the Silk Road, then the old legends (Penglai, Kunlun, the Dragon Palace, the Moon Palace and more). 31 new places up to 450 focus hours, each with its own drawn map, a story, a "did you know" and Zenko's line.
  - Every place has a small quiz: answer it for a gold seal in your **passport**, a stamp page for each region.
  - Places unlock rewards: travel outfits (snow coat, bingata, furisode, hanbok, hanfu, traveller's cloak, hagoromo), speech-bubble styles, garden decorations (deer, sea torii, maple, Korean lantern, pine, camel, moon rabbit, stone kitsune), sidebar scenes and new accent colours. Pick them in Customize.
- **Many more ema badges.** 61 now: 15 come in bronze, silver and gold tiers (hours, sessions, flashcards, fish, places and more), 16 new ones, and 12 secret ones that show as "?" until you find them. Click an earned plaque to pin up to 3 to the sidebar. Filters for All, Earned, Tiers and Secret.
- **Her speech bubble grows with her.** At Large and Huge sizes the bubble, its text and buttons scale up too (Customize > Speech bubble can turn this off).
- **Town square (new page).** One shared chat room for everyone using Zenko, online only. Pick a nickname (it shows with a short tag like #7fe7). Links, phone numbers, emails and social handles are removed, rude words are hidden, there's a 15-second slow mode, and you can right-click a message to report or block. Three reports hide a message for everyone. It uses the free ntfy.sh relay, so there's nothing to set up (Settings > Town square can point it at another ntfy server). It's off until you press Go online.
- **Zenko Browser link.** Zenko and the browser now see each other: a light in her sidebar (and a fox button in the browser) glows when both are open. She shares your upcoming deadlines, today's plan and ema reminders with the browser's new tab. Things you send from the browser's right-click Zenko menu arrive here: summaries (in her bubble and Chat, with "Save to notes"), flashcards, "quiz me on this page" cards, and dates as Upcoming deadlines or ema reminders. When you settle on a page in the browser she may notice what you're reading (at most every 10 minutes, never during a focus session; Settings can turn this off).
- New chat commands: "go fishing", "koi book", "passport", "badges", "town square", "browser".

## v2026.11.24-zenko21.1

## v2026.11.24-zenko21

Zenko 21 rebuilds the Notes tab and makes her look sharper. Your saved data and settings carry over.

- **Notes tab, rebuilt.**
  - **Scratchpad**: type anything, in a rush, with no headings. When you leave the Notes page or close the window, Zenko follows the topic line by line and files each part into the right subject notebook (French bits into French, Bio bits into Bio, and so on). A line like "Chem" or "Bio notes" starts a new subject straight away. "File it now" and "Undo last filing" buttons, and a live "Will file: French (3 lines), Bio (2 lines)" preview. Anything she can't place goes to "Unsorted". The draft is saved as you type, so nothing is lost if Zenko closes.
  - **Notebooks**: one per IB subject (Biology HL, Chemistry HL, Math AA SL, French ab initio SL, Psychology SL, English Lang & Lit HL), with folders you can open and close, pages you can read and edit, New page, New folder, Open and Delete (Delete moves things to a .bin folder, so you can get them back).
  - **Import a folder**: pick any folder, for example your reMarkable export. Its folder structure is copied in. A folder called Bio, Chemistry, French and so on goes straight into that subject's notebook; everything else lands under Imported. Zenko can keep checking that folder (on start and every 15 minutes) and copy in new or changed files the same way. PDFs with typed text can be read inside the app; handwriting opens in your PDF viewer.
  - The reMarkable cloud reader moved to its own window (the reMarkable… button).
- **Sharper pixel art.** Every sprite (her on the desktop, the clock fox, the pixel icons and more) is now enlarged with the Scale2x/Scale3x pixel-art filters instead of plain blocky squares. The designs are the same, with many more pixels and smoother edges. Settings > Pixel art quality can switch it off.
- **Text no longer squishes.** The window has a minimum size (1120 x 700, or your screen if smaller), and labels, card titles, checkboxes and button rows that were wrapped while the window was narrow now spread back out when there is room again.
- **Half-hour free time.** The Upcoming clock ring is now 24 half-hour pieces, so each hour has two halves you can tap or drag on their own. There's also a "Free from [16:30] to [18:00]  Add as free time / Remove" picker under the clock. The planner fits a full session into each free hour and a 25-minute session into a lone free half hour. Your old free hours are converted automatically.
- **New fonts.** The app now uses **Zen Maru Gothic**, a soft, rounded Japanese typeface that stays very easy to read, and page and card titles use **Zen Old Mincho**, a calm brush-style serif that fits the shrine look. Both are free (SIL Open Font License) and come inside zenko.py. On Windows they are loaded for Zenko only, so nothing is installed and no admin rights or PowerShell are needed. Text is sized up slightly so small hints stay readable. You can switch the titles back in Settings > Pixel art and fonts, or choose another body font under Look > Font.
- **A real page slide.** Switching tabs now slides the pages vertically: going down the sidebar the new page rises from below, going up it comes from above, and for a moment you can see both pages. The slide is timed by the clock, not by frames, and heavy redraws (the garden, the map, text fitting) wait until the page has landed, so it stays smooth. In tests the frames came about every 9 ms. Battery saver switches pages instantly.

## v2026.11.24-zenko20

Zenko 20 is the speed and ease update. Everything she could do before still works, and your saved data and settings are untouched.

- **Much faster.** The window now opens about 0.6 s after start instead of 1.6 s, and switching between pages is near instant (all 15 pages: 4.9 s before, 0.7 s now, measured on the same PC).
  - Every picture Zenko paints (her frames, tails, walk, the sidebar fox, the shelf, the muscle map, the wave banner) is remembered in `%APPDATA%\FoxBuddy\cache`, so it is painted only once, ever, instead of on every start.
  - Her next frames are pre-drawn in the background, so walking and poses never stutter the first time.
  - Pages get ready quietly in the background while you aren't touching anything, so clicking one shows it straight away (Settings > Speed and smoothness can turn this off).
  - When Zenko.exe runs a newer zenko.py from an update, it now compiles it once and reuses that (0.57 s down to 0.06 s per start).
- **Calmer on your PC.** Animations slow down when the window is hidden or you're in another app, and nothing is redrawn unless it changed. New **Motion** choice in Customize > Look and feel: Auto (calmer on battery), Smooth, Balanced or Battery saver.
- **Quick search (Ctrl+K)**, or click the search bar at the top: type a few letters to jump to any page, any settings card (it scrolls there and glows), a study book ("bio", "chem"), a theme or accent, or an action like box breathing, the periodic table, the calculator, a focus session or a break. Anything else you type can go straight to Chat.
- **Shortcuts**: Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 open the sidebar pages in order, Ctrl+, opens Settings, Alt+Left goes back to the page you were on.
- **Arrange the sidebar** (Customize > Sidebar pages): tick which pages show and move them up or down. Hidden pages are still one Ctrl+K away. There's also a compact sidebar option.
- **Accent colours** (Customize > Look and feel): Gold, Torii, Sakura, Jade, Sky, Wisteria or Moonlight on top of any fox theme, or keep the theme's own.
- Small polish: the Home button glow, tips and the chat "thinking" dots no longer repaint every frame, and the search bar fits its text.
